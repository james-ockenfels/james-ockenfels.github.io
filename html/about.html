Hey, I'm James, an aspiring IT and cybersecurity professional based in Ottumwa, Iowa. I'm currently finishing my senior year of high school while simultaneously completing an Associate degree in Cybersecurity and Systems Administration at Indian Hills Community College.

I work as an IT intern, where day-to-day I troubleshoot hardware, repair Chromebooks, assist with network infrastructure, and keep technical operations running smoothly. It's given me real hands-on experience that's hard to get any other way.

Outside of school and work, I spend a lot of time on personal projects, building hardware like my ESP32-based Flipper Zero clone, writing Python scripts, making games in Unity, and documenting everything on this site's blog. I got into tech because I genuinely enjoy figuring out how things work, and that curiosity hasn't slowed down.

My goal is to keep growing in the IT field, earn my CompTIA certifications, and eventually work full-time in cybersecurity or systems administration.
